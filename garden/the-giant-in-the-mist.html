The Giant in the Mist

Thinking of using these notes for drafting stories. See First Garden Note

I was walking by myself along the road of Brunswick. Yellow light from the streetlamps carved beams through the air. The mist was out tonight. It had rained all day and the moment I stepped outside all I could feel was the damp seeping into my skin. The houses were stark white, like the bones of the earth
