Dan The Space Wizard

October 28, 2026

Dan is a project that emerged because I was feeling burnt out with Hat Guy and I knew from my experience at Illumination that I needed to get better at drawing, in addition to my storytelling. So I created this sort of space-jelly-astronaut character and put him in bizarre situations. When I’m feeling like I need a break, I draw potential panels for Dan. They are viewable on this site and on my more general instagram account
