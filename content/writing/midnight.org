#+type: story

#+description: Traveling through dreams

I traversed the black ice of dreams. It was easy to slip up here. To fall into the dark desires of a slumbering priest, or the empty philosophies of a half-awake teenager. It was the gaps you needed to watch out for. The icy breaks of failure. One slip of the foot could send you catapulting into nightmares beyond the reach of remembering.

But I was sure-footed. I'd traveled these paths many times, and would again. I skipped over the sweet dreams of a child, and danced across those fiery thoughts of a blacksmith. The murderous thoughts of kings and the cavernous contemplation of a fly were no strangers to my footsteps. I was Miriam the Dreamwalker, and no sleeping mind was far from my reach.

At long last, I came across the dream of my prey. He was a small thing, no more than a boy. He didn't deserve this. But money was money, and lately it was also something I desperately needed. I reached out to his mind carefully, extending a branch of myself like a tree in a storm. I wrapped myself around the leaves of his thoughts, the little grey matter, the neurons trapping themselves languorously. I was surprised. He was like me? I felt again and... yes. Untrained, but a Dreamwalker all the same. I must be cautious. I reached out carefully so as not to wake him from his dream. I glanced at it as I worked. He was with an old woman. His grandmother? He must have a fondness for her to be present this deep in his subconscious. She shouted, and he looked ashamed.

/Hmmm. maybe not/

This was difficult work, and I was careful as I extracted his origins. I stretched them out until they lay in a spool before me. This always made me think of the Fates, the oldest Dreamwalkers, long dead. Three women deciding fortune with thread.  I imagined my walks to be similar to theirs. I was the modern Lachesis, shaping futures with a snip of thread. I found the necessary portion of his identity and snipped it away, rolling it carefully underneath his psyche. I began to retrace my steps, going back the way I came, but when I turned around the only thing I could find was darkness in my path.

/What do you think you're doing/. I flipped back around, shocked to find the boy staring at me. He looked mildly annoyed.

/Umm.... I.../,

/You shouldn't be here. This is my grandmothers house, and she didn't invite you./ His eyes glowed with morpheic power and he reached out with a strength that was impossible for one his age. My eyes widened and I tried desperately to make my escape.

I sprinted through the darkness but his eyes and shadowy hands chased me. And before I could reach out to my master, to ask what dark figure he had asked me to Sever, I felt a vicelike grip around my throat, and all went dark.
