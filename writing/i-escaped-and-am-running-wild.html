I escaped

November 2, 2026

I managed to escape my imprisonment and am now hiding in a homemade shelter in the dark forest.

I think Frinklepuff and his fellow gnomes lost the trail 2 days ago, but I’ve been laying low to be safe. I’m always nervous that I might see a pointy red hat turn the corner. If I am captured again they might by chance discover my weakness and put me in a hole so deep and dank that no one will ever find me.

I saw Wildwood. It was great! All around 10/10 film. Very dark though. I was not expecting it to be quite as gory and violent as it ended up being. But fantastic. Real Narnia / Alice in Wonderland vibes.

Anyways, I’ve made friends with one of the dryads here. She’s been keeping me hidden under her branches, which is the shelter I mentioned before. I may voyage out for food in a few hours if I don’t hear any spritely giggling.

Until then,

Jack
