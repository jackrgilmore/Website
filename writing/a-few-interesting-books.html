A Few Interesting Books

October 25, 2026

Recently I’ve gotten really into weird fiction / cosmic horror as a genre, so here are some recommendations:

  1. There is no Antimemetics Division by qntm
    • This book was originally a series of short stories from the SMP Foundation. It’s fantastic.
  2. The Fisherman by John Langan
  3. The Hike by Drew Magary
    • Really bizzare story of a guy who tries to go on a short hike which ends up lasting decades
  4. Blacktail by Scott Hawkins
    • What if God wasn’t human?
  5. Slow Gods by Claire North
  6. City of Stairs by Robert Jackson Bennet
  7. A Short Stay in Hell by Steven L. Peck

They are amazing! Also, if you haven’t already, read the Cradle series by Will Wight.
