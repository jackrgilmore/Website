I accidentally set a tree on fire

November 10, 2026

So… the dryad is not happy with me.

I had built a little fire pit out of some cleared ground and small rocks, and I managed to get some embers going using nearby twigs . Unfortunately I’ve never been into those survival videos some people watch, and the pit didn’t properly protect the fire from spreading. I watched with horror as it climbed up the tree and Pythaea (that’s the dryad’s name) came out screeching at me, her twiglike limbs extending into claws. I tried to reason with her but roots extended from her mouth in long fangs. I tried to explain what happened but she glared like she was going to beat me over the head with my own torn off legs, so I made a tactical retreat towards the sound of running water.

Unfortunately, I have two exams this week. One in Renaissance Drama and one in Italian. Not really sure how to study for the Drama exam and the Italian one is a bit intimidating.

Anyways, I’ve found the river and I think I see a glint of metal flashing from deeper within the forest. Not sure if that’s Frinklepuff or some other dark denizen of this strange world. I’ll go check it out.

Until next time,

Jack
