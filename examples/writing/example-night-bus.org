* Route 9
The night bus ran from the hospital to the harbor and back, nine stops each way,
and Dell had driven it for six years without once having a full bus.

Tonight there were three passengers. A nurse going home. A man with a cello
case. A girl of maybe ten, alone, sitting very straight in the front seat with
her ticket held out in both hands.

"Where to?" said Dell.

"The end," said the girl.

* The End of the Line
The end of the line was the harbor, and the harbor at two in the morning was
fog and gulls and the long slow groan of the ferry ropes.

The nurse got off at stop four. The man with the cello got off at seven. The
girl stayed where she was, ticket still held out, until the doors hissed open on
the dark water.

"Someone meeting you?" Dell asked.

She pointed. At the end of the pier, a lantern was swinging, coming closer.
